IMPORTANT FORMAT (all 4):

- Portrait card, aspect ratio 5:8. The card artwork must fill the ENTIRE image edge to edge. No paper background, no table, no shadow, no mockup frame, no margin around the card.
- Consistent style across all images so the remaining 46 cards can be made identically later.

STYLE: modernized classic Korean hwatu (화투) — the look Korean players instantly recognize from real plastic hwatu cards.

- Faithful to traditional hwatu iconography and composition for each month so the month is recognizable at a glance even at small size (60px wide).
- Bold black outlines, flat saturated colors (crimson red, black, pine green, ivory, small gold accents), subtle glossy plastic highlight.
- Simple, iconic composition with large shapes. NOT a detailed painting, NOT a sunset landscape.
- Absolutely NO Japanese motifs: no torii gates, no Japanese shrines, no kimono, no Japanese text.
- No text, letters or numbers anywhere, except a small red circle with the Hanja "光" in one corner of bright (광) cards.

CARD CONTENT:

- 01_gwang: ivory/white background, big red sun (circle) at top, white crane with red crown standing at bottom, dark green pine branches framing. "光" mark top-left corner.
- 08_gwang: top half plain ivory/white sky with a huge white full moon is replaced by: deep red sky top area with a big white full moon, bottom half a large black rounded hill with sparse grass blades (the classic 공산 design). "光" mark top-left.
- 12_ssangpi: Korean-style rain card: bold diagonal rain streaks, dark storm cloud, a willow branch hanging, a stylized red-and-black ornamental drum shape in the center (classic hwatu December junk design, abstract, not a gate). Two small marks in a corner indicating double value are NOT needed.
- back.png: deep crimson red card back with a subtle repeating traditional Korean pattern (taegeuk-free, use 卍-free cloud/lattice 문살 pattern), thin gold inner border, glossy plastic feel.
